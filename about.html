DJ Disco Dan

The music man · The chatty one

Dan grew up surrounded by the sounds and influences of the 60s, 70s and 80s – a golden age of music that helped shape his lifelong love of great tunes. From timeless classics and forgotten gems to the songs that bring back a thousand memories, he has a real appreciation for music and the stories behind it.

A proud Beatles fan with a soft spot for all sorts of musical discoveries, Dan loves sharing the background, memories and little bits of music history that make a song special. Expect a friendly voice, plenty to say, a few stories along the way and the sort of chat that makes you feel part of the show.

Whether he's digging out an old favourite or dropping a tune you haven't heard in years, DJ Disco Dan is here to keep the memories alive and the music playing.

🐦‍🔥🎤

Mr Phoenix

The musical wildcard · King of the wind-up

Mr Phoenix brings his own unmistakable personality to D&P FREEBIRD. He's got an absolute obsession with Nickelback, a big love of Glee and an enthusiasm for musical surprises that can take a show in just about any direction.

One minute you might get a rock favourite, the next a show tune or something completely unexpected. Phoenix loves keeping listeners guessing, mixing things up and finding just the right moment to drop a musical curveball.

Add his witty wind-up banter, cheeky sense of humour and talent for keeping Dan on his toes, and you never quite know what's coming next – which is exactly how he likes it.

🪩🐦‍🔥 WHY D&P FREEBIRD?

The D stands for Dan. The P stands for Phoenix. Two presenters, two personalities and two very different musical journeys, brought together under one name.

FREEBIRD represents the freedom to let the music fly. We don't want to be boxed into one decade, one genre or one predictable playlist. From the golden oldies to rock anthems, pop favourites, show tunes, hidden gems and unexpected surprises, the music can go wherever the moment takes it.

And that's what MUSIC WITHOUT BOUNDARIES means to us: great music, good company, plenty of personality and room for every kind of listener. Different tastes, shared microphones and absolutely no musical boundaries.

D&P FREEBIRD – RADIO & ENTERTAINMENT. Two names, one station, and a whole lot of music still to come!
