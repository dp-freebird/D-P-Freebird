DJ DISCO DAN

LIVE WIRE is an independent music and broadcasting brand built around a lifelong love of great music, live shows and bringing listeners together.

DJ Disco Dan grew up with the sounds of the 60s, 70s and 80s, with a particular love of the Beatles, timeless classics, forgotten gems and the stories behind the songs.

Expect a friendly voice, plenty of musical memories, requests, cheeky conversation and a soundtrack that moves across the decades.
