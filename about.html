MY LOVE OF MUSIC

I'm DJ Disco Dan, and music has been part of my life for as long as I can remember. I have a real passion for the songs of the 60s, 70s and 80s, the artists and stories behind them, and the memories that a familiar tune can bring flooding back. The Beatles are a particular favourite, but I love exploring music across the decades and sharing forgotten gems alongside the classics.

For me, broadcasting is about more than playing records. It's about connecting with people, sharing memories, having a laugh, taking requests and creating that feeling of good company wherever listeners happen to be.

FROM LIVE WIRE ENTERTAINMENT TO LIVE WIRE

The original LIVE WIRE began as a joint venture under the name Live Wire Entertainment. It was the starting point for an idea built around music, broadcasting and bringing people together.

As the project evolved, I decided it was time to take the brand in a new direction: a shorter, sharper name with a more distinctive identity. That is how LIVE WIRE came back into its own — an independent brand with a fresh luxury look, led by bold black, metallic gold and rich ruby red.

The new strapline says exactly what it stands for: MUSIC • LIVE • BROADCASTING. The name has its roots in the original idea, but this is the next chapter: focused on great music, live shows and the listeners who make it worthwhile.
